# Horizon

## Vision
A unified web application that consolidates all aspects of your investment research, analysis, and trading workflow into a single, locally-hosted dashboard. Access from Mac or iPhone via Tailscale.

Combines:
- **Trading journal** (6 indicators + entry/position sizing rules)
- **Stock research** (checklist, notes, trade/invest decision)
- **Valuation** (equity multiple method, company comparisons)
- **Trading diary** (execution tracking, trade outcomes)
- **Smart money tracking** (guru holdings, QoQ changes)
